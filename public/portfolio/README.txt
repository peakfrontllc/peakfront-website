Peakfront Company Portfolio
===========================

HTML (interactive, best for PDF export):
  Open public/portfolio/index.html in your browser
  Click "Save as PDF / Print" and choose "Save as PDF"

PNG pages (generated):
  public/portfolio/pages/01-cover.png
  public/portfolio/pages/02-about.png

Regenerate PNG cover pages:
  node scripts/generate-portfolio.mjs

Full 7-page portfolio is in index.html — use Print to PDF for the complete document.
